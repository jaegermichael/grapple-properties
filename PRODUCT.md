# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Zimbabwe-based and diaspora property buyers, sellers, landlords, corporate occupiers, diplomatic missions, and investors evaluating high-value real estate in Harare. They need to identify credible opportunities and begin a confidential conversation with a locally informed adviser.

## Product Purpose

Grapple Properties presents residential, commercial, rental, and land opportunities across Harare and converts qualified interest into property enquiries, private viewings, valuations, and advisory engagements.

## Positioning

The product differentiates through property-specific due diligence and local operating intelligence: title status, water resilience, solar capacity, security, zoning, and street-level suburb knowledge are treated as decision-critical facts rather than afterthoughts.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare listings by property type and suburb, inspect accommodation and land-area specifications, evaluate infrastructure resilience, and contact the Karigamombe Center office by form, phone, or WhatsApp. Many users may be making decisions remotely from outside Zimbabwe.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Next.js 16 application with responsive marketing, listing, detail, services, about, and contact routes.
- Preserve current routes, listing filters, property detail data, enquiry interactions, office contact details, and Zimbabwe/Harare terminology.
- Property and company claims in the current repository are treated as supplied content, not independently verified facts.
- Remote property photography is currently sourced from Unsplash.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Grapple Properties name, Harare headquarters, green identity, and the line “defining success and leading you home.” The voice should feel discreet, direct, locally knowledgeable, and rigorous rather than flamboyant.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains eight illustrative property records, service descriptions, neighbourhood coverage, contact details, agent profiles, and testimonials. No separately supplied logo package, original property photography, registration certificate, or independently verified performance evidence is present.

## Product Principles

- Make due diligence visible and understandable.
- Let property evidence lead before brand claims.
- Serve remote and local decision-makers with equal clarity.
- Make confidential human contact easy at every high-intent moment.
- Present Zimbabwean place and infrastructure realities with specificity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public website should support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, and responsive layouts from small mobile screens through large desktop displays.
